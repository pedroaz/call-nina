This is a custom source-available license, not PolyForm and not an OSI-approved open-source license. The license text controls; this document explains its scope and provenance.

Independent personal self-study is free. Personal learners may inspect, run and modify covered material and share it free of charge under the notice, source and modification conditions in `LICENSE`. Professional teaching, institution-operated or assigned learning, organizational deployments and hosted learning services require separate written permission for covered material, even when offered without charge. There is no general educational or nonprofit exception. A teacher learning independently at home qualifies as a personal learner; a school assigning Nina exercises to a class does not. Anyone may inspect the source, view the public website and share its links.

**The new restrictions do not apply to previously MIT-licensed material.** The repository at [`6d83af64512bb0853b007a89f9f71b16f16d5143`](https://github.com/pedroaz/call-nina/tree/6d83af64512bb0853b007a89f9f71b16f16d5143) and its ancestors remain available under MIT, including commercial and institutional use and redistribution. This also preserves MIT permissions for material previously distributed elsewhere or on parallel branches. An unchanged copy of that code does not become restricted just because it is distributed after adoption; only new protectable contributions first offered under the new terms are covered. Most application functionality at adoption therefore remains usable under MIT. Reusing old material does not require buying new permission.

An updated distribution may contain new covered portions, old MIT portions and third-party portions. The root license records this boundary and retains the exact operative historic MIT text, including `Copyright (c) 2026 Open Deutsch contributors`, plus the earlier Call Nina attribution. The new license does not revoke earlier grants or promise exclusivity over the existing product. A copyright notice or modified file alone cannot establish which new elements are protectable.

Free personal BYOK carries no Nina license fee; provider charges and requirements remain separate. Product plans retain BYOK and eligible personal Codex access for Premium personal; Teachers and Enterprise plan managed access without BYOK or personal subscription-runtime access. Direct BYOK and managed access are planned features, not presently delivered by this licensing change. Product/service plans confer no extra copyright permission, and a provider subscription confers no Nina professional license. This change implements no billing, entitlement enforcement or service agreement.

## Selection rationale and official sources

The following primary sources were consulted on 2026-09-29. The fit assessment is a project licensing decision, not a legal opinion on enforceability in every jurisdiction.

| Candidate | Relevant terms and fit |
| --- | --- |
| [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit) | Permits unrestricted use, modification, distribution and sale subject to notices. It remains applicable to the historic material but cannot impose the requested personal/institutional boundary. |
| [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0) | Allows noncommercial uses, changes and distribution, and expressly permits educational institutions and other listed organizations regardless of funding. Its organizational exception conflicts with this task. |
| [PolyForm Strict 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/strict/1.0.0) | Retains that organizational exception and excludes distribution and changes. It does not meet either the institution restriction or the desired personal modification/sharing permission. |
| [Business Source License 1.1](https://mariadb.com/bsl11/) | Grants non-production use and requires transition to a qualifying change license by the change date or fourth anniversary. Those broad permissions and mandatory transition differ from the chosen boundary. |
| Call Nina Personal Learning Source License 1.0 | Selected custom terms explicitly distinguish independent learners from professional/organizational use, allow personal adaptations and free source/binary sharing, and preserve existing rights. No claim of standardized legal review or compatibility certification is made. |

Restrictions on professional/organizational fields of use mean the new terms are **source available**, not open source under the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). The repository metadata uses npm's [custom-license file reference](https://docs.npmjs.com/cli/v11/configuring-npm/package-json/#license), `SEE LICENSE IN LICENSE`, rather than a misleading sole MIT identifier or an invented SPDX identifier. Private workspace manifests without a separate license declaration remain subject to the root file; they do not create separate licensing grants.

## Modifications, distribution and contributions

For new covered material, free source forks and binary redistribution must include the entire license, attribution and applicable third-party notices, mark modifications and supply corresponding source for distributed binaries. Modifications to covered material must be offered under the same terms. These requirements do not narrow independently licensed MIT or third-party material. Commercial redistribution or use in a professional/organizational learning service needs separate written permission. Contact [Pedro Azevedo Minutetnag](mailto:pedro.azvm@gmail.com) to request it; requesting permission is not itself a grant.

External contributors retain their rights. Submission alone is not a copyright assignment or permission for commercial relicensing. Before incorporating an external contribution, the maintainer and contributor must explicitly agree in writing on its license and the additional rights needed for separate professional/organizational licensing, with third-party provenance identified. An external contribution under personal-use terms alone does not give the maintainer those broader rights. This policy grants no rights retroactively on behalf of contributors.
