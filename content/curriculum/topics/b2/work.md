  explanation: >-
    Konjunktiv II can express courteous requests, advice or retrospective
    criticism: Sie hätten früher nachfragen können. Distinguish this from a
    factual past. Modal Perfekt uses an infinitive group: hat prüfen müssen.
    Causal noun phrases use wegen/aufgrund + genitive; because-clauses require a
    different sentence structure. Soften retrospective criticism with phrasing
    and pause before a proposed remedy.


    Instrumental, causal and consequential noun phrases use different
    prepositions: mithilfe + genitive, aufgrund + genitive, infolge + genitive.
    Do not substitute one solely because its case matches. Discourse particles
    such as doch, ja and mal shape shared assumptions and tone; they cannot be
    translated mechanically or inserted into formal letters at random. Compare a
    neutral request with a familiar one containing mal; let context determine
    the register.
