  explanation: >-
    Learn nouns with their article and plural: der Bruder → die Brüder, die
    Schwester → die Schwestern. Ein/eine introduces one person or thing;
    kein/keine negates a noun. Mein/meine marks possession and follows the noun
    gender: mein Bruder, meine Schwester. Plural has no indefinite article.
    Contrast ei in meine with long ie in sie. Stress the first syllable in
    Bruder.


    Separable verbs form a bracket: Ich stehe um sieben auf. In questions the
    prefix also stays last. A modal such as können is conjugated; the main verb
    is an infinitive at the end: Ich kann morgen kommen. With time first, the
    verb stays second: Morgen komme ich. Stress AUF in aufstehen. Contrast ich
    and acht without adding an extra vowel.


    Use am with dates: am dritten Mai. Ordinal endings after am are -en.
    Personal pronouns replace accusative objects: der Drucker → ihn, die Liste →
    sie, das Formular → es. Time phrases such as vor dem Treffen and nach der
    Pause use dative after vor/nach. Contrast the initial sounds in vier and
    wir; German v in vier sounds like f.
