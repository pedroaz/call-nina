  explanation: >-
    Separate habit from a completed event: Ich arbeite morgens; gestern habe ich
    abends gearbeitet. Modal verbs form a bracket with an infinitive. Use
    war/hatte for past states and Perfekt for conversational events. A time
    expression can occupy position one without moving the conjugated verb from
    position two. Stress the verb root in gearbeitet; keep unstressed prefixes
    light.


    Weil gives a reason and puts the verb last. Compare denn with normal word
    order. Indefinite pronouns can replace nouns: Hast du einen Stift? Ja, ich
    habe einen. Pronoun endings retain gender and case. State a reason before
    negotiating a new option rather than simply declining. At the end of a word,
    d is pronounced like t. Compare Geld and Gelder.
