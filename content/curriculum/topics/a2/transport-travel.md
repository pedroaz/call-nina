lessonFoundation:
  explanation: >-
    Compare two choices with comparative + als; use so … wie for equality.
    Superlatives use am …sten. In a genitive noun phrase masculine/neuter
    articles become des and nouns often add -(e)s: die Öffnungszeiten des
    Museums. Direction and fixed location still require different cases after
    two-way prepositions. Stress the first component of compound nouns:
    SCHLOSSgarten, STADTmuseum.


    Damit expresses a purpose and puts the verb last, including when the subject
    changes: Ich spreche langsam, damit du mich verstehst. Countries with
    articles need case endings: in die Schweiz, in der Schweiz. Without an
    article use nach for direction: nach Deutschland. Practise r after a vowel
    in Uhr and before a vowel in reisen. Aim for clear contrasts, not a
    particular accent.


    Distinguish departure from arrival. Statt introduces a replacement in the
    announcement. Although a connection leaves earlier, it may arrive later;
    compare the complete route before choosing.
